# Writing a voice that sounds like a person

Tonally hands the voice to an AI model as instructions for every reply. Whatever the voice
lists, the model reaches for - so a voice that is vague, or full of catchphrases, gives replies
that read as machine-written. These are the patterns that give a reply away, and how to keep them
out of the voice.

## Keep out of the voice

- **Catchphrase lists.** "the real bottleneck is", "nobody talks about", "plot twist", "in this
  economy". Listed as vocabulary, they turn up in nearly every reply. Keep `vocabulary` to a few
  plain words the person really uses (`yeah`, `depends`, `in practice`) - five at most.
- **Poster lines.** Tidy mirrored one-liners ("hire slow, fire fast") and "it's not X, it's Y" /
  "not X but Y". They are the clearest sign of AI writing.
- **Labels and praise as openers.** "Pro tip:", "Hot take:", "Underrated:", "Worth adding:",
  "Great point", "Love this", "Great question", or "This." as a whole reply.
- **Invented personal results.** "saved me months", "10x'd our revenue", "grew to 50k". The model
  would claim them in the user's name. Personal experience stays general: "we tried that", "most
  teams I have seen".
- **Stock words.** game-changer, delve, leverage, elevate, unlock, journey, crucial, "in today's
  world", "let's dive in".
- **Stock meme formats and habit emojis.** "nobody:", "the audacity", "me pretending", and 💀 😭
  out of habit.
- **Em dashes (—).** Tonally replaces them with a hyphen anyway; write a hyphen, a comma or a full
  stop.

## Put into the voice

- **Rules a stranger could follow.** "one or two sentences, lowercase starts are fine, no full
  stop at the end" beats "casual".
- **Uneven, natural rhythm.** Contractions, the odd fragment, sentences of different lengths.
- **A detail from the post.** Tell the voice to pick up something specific from the post it
  answers rather than state a general truth.
- **Questions only when they are real.** No question tacked on at the end to get engagement.
- **Varied openings.** Not every reply starts with "I", with the advice, or with the same word.

## Examples

Write 5 to 8 new example replies that show the rhythm. Vary length and opening. Each must make
sense as an answer to some post, under 280 characters, with no links, @mentions or hashtags.
