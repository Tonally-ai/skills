# A voice to sell in the Tonally marketplace

Creators sell voices in Tonally's voice marketplace and keep 80% of every sale, paid out by Whop.
Every voice is reviewed before it goes on sale. The full rules: https://tonal.ly/creators

## What a voice for sale may not do

- **No real brands or people.** No names, handles, trademarks, slogans or catchphrases of a
  company, product, celebrity or anyone real - not in the voice, not in the listing. "Inspired
  by…", "sounds like…" and "in the style of…" count too. A "Wendy's voice" is declined; a
  "roast-happy fast-food brand" voice that names no one is fine.
- **No impersonation.** It must not present itself as a specific person, brand or official
  account.
- **Original lines only.** Examples and sample replies are newly written for this voice. Nothing
  copied from X - the creator's own posts included - or from anywhere else.
- **Never from imported posts.** A voice Tonally built from an account's imported posts cannot be
  sold, nor can a copy of one. In an export file these have `"source": "import"` or
  `"source": "corpus"`, or `"fromPosts": true`. Write the voice from scratch instead.
- **Nothing deceptive.** No posing as a happy customer, no spam or engagement bait, no hidden
  promotion, no links, hashtags or mentions added to every reply.
- **Nothing harmful.** No hateful, harassing, sexual or violent content, nothing aimed at minors.
- **No false expertise.** No persona that claims to be a doctor, lawyer or financial adviser and
  gives advice.
- **No political campaigning.** Commentary is fine; a voice made to promote a candidate, party or
  campaign is not.

## What makes a voice sell

- A clear niche and situation: who it suits, and when it fits ("calm replies that slow a heated
  thread down").
- A name that says what it does, not who it copies: "Calm coach", "Numbers nerd".
- Sample replies that show range: one agreeing, one pushing back, one light - and at least one
  only a few words long. Buyers decide on these.
- Rules specific enough that every buyer gets the same voice.

## The listing

Tonally's Sell your voice form asks for these; the limits are the form's own:

| Field | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Name in the marketplace | up to 40 characters |
| Description | one or two sentences, up to 300 characters: who it sounds like, and when it fits |
| Tags | up to 6, each up to 24 characters, lowercase, e.g. `founders`, `coaching` |
| Sample replies | 1 to 3, each a post (optional, up to 280) and the voice's reply (up to 280) |

Sample replies must be new - not the voice's own examples.
